Sebastian Brant's *Ship of Fools* appeared in Basel in 1494 and became, within a few years, the most successful German-language book of its time.
The copy shown here, from the Basel Charterhouse, belongs to the edition Johann Bergmann de Olpe printed on 3 March 1495.

For this project it is the central volume: it alone carries a fully worked system of border strips – narrow,
re-usable ornamental blocks framing the text on the left and the right.
For 208 of its 328 pages, the data records which strip type stands on which side.

Two things repay attention while reading: how the woodcuts comment on the text rather than illustrate it –
and how the same blocks reappear at other places in the two Latin editions of 1497.
