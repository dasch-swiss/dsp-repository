The commentary on the pericopes by Guillelmus Parisiensis, printed by Michael Furter on 28 July 1491, is among the working books of the corpus: a preacher's tool,
densely furnished with small woodcuts.

It is here that two pairs are documented in which the same block was printed twice within one volume – on e3r and e4r, and again on e6r and e7r.
The annotators' markings sit on the scan and can be shown while paging through the volume.
