# The Deposit Area is two capabilities, identity and editor

The Deposit Area's modulith (ADR-0003) is composed of two capabilities. **identity** owns accounts, sessions, one-time login codes, the mail that carries them and the depositor list. **editor** owns the draft, the submission, the review, entity proposals, approved records and their publication to Git, as one lifecycle aggregate. The capability keeps the name `editor` (ADR-0002); "deposition" is the platform's word for the act of depositing and names no crate, directory or capability (`areas/deposit/editor/CONTEXT.md`, Flagged ambiguities). Two rather than more: review, proposals and approved records stay inside editor because approve is one transaction across `submissions`, `review_rounds`, `approved_records` and `entity_proposals`, and splitting any of them off would trade that atomic transition for the eventual consistency ADR-0003 prescribes between capabilities, for an extraction nobody plans.

Two is the count today, not a ceiling: the editor is to grow into a small virtual research environment, and data modelling and upload are to arrive as capabilities of their own, each a directory beside these two (ADR-0003). This record fixes only the first two.

## The shape

- `areas/deposit/server` — `deposit-server`, the area's composition root (ADR-0002): configuration, observability, opening the two databases, constructing every adapter and injecting it, assembling the router. It holds no capability's handler, view or SQL. What it does hold is response glue that belongs to no capability: the 404 fallback for unrouted paths (rendered with `deposit-shell`), the same-origin check on state-changing requests (`csrf.rs`), static assets, and the page-URL normalizer for browser telemetry (`page_url.rs`), built from the two web crates' route tables. Its tests that need both capabilities' stores (`cross_capability.rs`, `removed_account.rs`) live here because no capability crate may name the other's store.
- `areas/deposit/shell` — `deposit-shell`, the area's document shell: `page`, the header with the viewer's name and the sign-out form, the footer, the 403 and 404 pages, the sign-in link (`/login`, `login_url`) with `safe_next`, the area's one copy of the open-redirect allowlist, the RDU-only text and the "temporarily unavailable" problem page. Both web crates redirect a signed-out request to `/login?next=…`, show the RDU-only text on a 403 and render the problem page, and neither may depend on the other, so these sit in the shell. A `fn -> Markup` library depending on `mosaic-tiles` and `shared-*` only, never on a capability crate. The routes it names (`/projects`, `/review`, `/depositors`, `/login`, `/logout`) are string literals: a coupling to both capabilities' route tables that no compiler checks.
- `areas/deposit/identity/{core,ports,store,web}`:
  - `identity-core`: User, Role, Session, LoginCode, the login-flow rules and send caps, its repository ports, the `Mailer` trait, cookie parsing and the session secret.
  - `identity-ports`: what identity needs from the editor, the `OpenWork` port with two reads, open drafts and open submissions. The depositor-removal confirmation lists them, so RDU sees what removing the account leaves without an author, and says which of the two reads failed. `editor-store` implements it as `LiveOpenWork` beside its tables, in the same consumer-defined direction as `editor-ports` (ADR-0003).
  - `identity-store`: SQLite for `users`, `user_shortcodes`, `sessions`, `login_codes`, `mail_sends`; the SMTP and console mailers; the `Live*` adapters implementing `editor-ports`. `store` holds identity's infrastructure adapters, SQLite and SMTP alike, behind traits `identity-core` declares: this area's reading of ADR-0003's "store".
  - `identity-web`: the login and sign-out pages, the depositor administration, their handlers and routes, its own `Authenticated` and `Rdu` extractors over its own session module, and the `Identity` facade (below).
- `areas/deposit/editor/{core,ports,store,web,pull-requests}`:
  - `editor-core`.
  - `editor-ports` (below).
  - `editor-store`: SQLite for `drafts`, `submissions`, `review_rounds`, `approved_records`, `entity_proposals`.
  - `editor-web`: the views, and the handlers and routes for sections, review, entities, projects, approved and `/api/v1`; its own `Authenticated(Actor)` and `Rdu` extractors over `editor-ports`; the `Editor` facade (below).
  - `editor-pull-requests`: the binary that opens the pull request (`areas/deposit/ADR-0001`).
- `pull-requests` is a role of this area beside ADR-0003's general roles: a capability's binary that talks to the outside world and holds no web or storage code. Its allowed edges are its own capability's crates and `shared-*`.

### The root reaches each capability through a facade

`identity-web` exposes `Identity` (its configuration; at start, `ensure_rdu`, the mailer and the expiry sweep; its router). `editor-web` exposes `Editor` (its configuration; at start, the published set, which it loads once and holds, the temporal tables, the agents and the startup reconcile; its router). Both are in the shape of `dpe-server`'s `Dpe`. `deposit-server` opens the two stores, constructs the adapters, starts `Identity` before `Editor`, and merges their routers. `Identity::start` failing ends the process; `Editor::start` returns no error. `deposit-server`'s dependencies are the two web crates, the two store crates, `deposit-shell` and `shared-telemetry`; it names no `core` crate and no `ports` crate, and receives the `ports` types through the store crates' adapters. ADR-0003 states the rule for every area.

## What crosses the boundary

- `editor-ports` declares an `Actor` (account id, display name, role, the folded shortcodes the account may reach, and the session's deadline, the earlier of its absolute expiry and its idle deadline) and two ports: an authenticator, taking the request's `Cookie` header and the current instant and answering `Option<Actor>`, and a user directory, taking account ids and answering display names. `editor-web` consumes both: the authenticator in its extractors, the directory in the review queue and the concurrent-save notice. The deadline is computed from identity's session, crosses the boundary in the `Actor`, and is not recomputed by editor: the editor's forms warn a reader who is about to be signed out.
- `identity-store` implements them as `LiveAuthenticator` and `LiveUserDirectory` over its own tables. The session's idle-timeout touch on `GET` stays inside `LiveAuthenticator`: it is identity's write on identity's table.
- Each capability's web crate owns its extractors. `identity-web`'s read its session module directly; `editor-web`'s call the port. There is no shared extractor and no authentication middleware (ADR-0003).
- Every account reference editor stores (`drafts.updated_by`, `submissions.submitted_by` and `reviewed_by`, `review_rounds.actor`, `approved_records.approved_by`, `entity_proposals.proposed_by` and `decided_by`) is an opaque `TEXT` id with no `REFERENCES` clause. A removed account is what the directory answering nothing renders as; `review_rounds.actor` keeps its value for the audit trail.
- The pull-request run and the `/api/v1` routes are editor's; the `/api/v1` routes are unauthenticated and never read a session.

## Two databases

Each capability opens its own SQLite file under `EDITOR_DB_DIR`, `identity.sqlite` and `editor.sqlite`, with its own migration series under its own `PRAGMA user_version` and its own single-writer pool; the in-memory variant is two named shared-cache databases. A store crate opens only its own file, so ADR-0003's "no cross-capability SQL" holds by structure rather than by review, and approve stays one transaction because every table it touches is editor's. Nothing irreplaceable is in either file (`areas/deposit/ADR-0001`). The backup note in `docs/src/editor/operations.md` names both files.

A file per capability rather than one file, because what separates the two is ownership, not size. One file is one `PRAGMA user_version`, so one migration series both capabilities edit and neither owns, and one connection on which `editor-store` can name `users` with nothing but review to say no: crate-edge visibility (ADR-0001) never closes that, since it checks crate edges and not SQL text. Two files make the boundary a runtime fact: a store crate holds one connection, and the other capability's tables do not exist on it. The cost is bounded. A SQLite file is a file in `EDITOR_DB_DIR`, not a server, so a third capability is a third file on the same volume under the same backup note, and the file count grows with the capability count and with nothing else: the modulith's shape (ADR-0003), not a sprawl. The only query it makes impossible is the cross-capability join ADR-0003 already forbids. Should the editor's database become the store of record (`areas/deposit/ADR-0001`), the split is what lets each capability's data be backed up, migrated and, if it comes to it, hosted on its own.

Configuration is loaded once at the root, and each capability receives the part it declares. Identity's terms are in `areas/deposit/identity/CONTEXT.md`.

## Considered Options

- **Two capabilities, identity and editor (chosen).**
- **One capability, the editor as it is** — rejected: accounts and sessions are the first thing the next capabilities (`media`, the data-model creator, data creation) need, and inside the editor they could only be reached through the editor's crates, the sibling import ADR-0003 forbids.
- **Three or more: review, entity proposals or approved records as capabilities of their own** — rejected: approve deletes the submission, writes the round, applies the proposal decisions and writes the approved record in one transaction; as separate capabilities each step would be its own transaction, consistent eventually, for an extraction nobody plans.
- **`deposition` as the capability's name** — rejected: `deposit`, `deposition` and `depositor` on adjacent doors say less than one of them.
- **One area-level `deposit-auth` crate holding one extractor for both web crates** — rejected: an extractor needs the session lookup, so the crate would depend on `identity-store` and every consumer on it transitively, the edge ADR-0003 forbids; behind a port it is the chosen shape with one crate more.
- **Authentication as a middleware in `deposit-server` putting an actor into request extensions** — rejected: the composition root would hold adapter logic, and the structure-level rule "a public handler is public in its signature" (`ARCH-MAP.md`) would go.
- **The shell in `deposit-server`, as DPE keeps it** — rejected: the handlers live in the capabilities' web crates and have to produce responses, so either each declares a shell port the root implements (view code at the composition root) or a root-level layer rewrites responses.
- **One shell per capability** — rejected: two copies of a header whose navigation spans both, drifting with no test.
- **A generic app-shell tile in `mosaic-tiles`** — *deferred*: it would cover DPE too and widens this decision into a Mosaic and DPE change; it comes back when Mosaic and DPE want one shell tile, and `deposit-shell` can then be replaced by it without touching a port.
- **One database file with the `REFERENCES` clauses dropped** — rejected: one `user_version` is one migration series that no capability owns, and any store crate could still read the other's tables (Two databases).
- **One file, each capability's migrations tracked in a table of its own instead of `user_version`** — rejected: it solves the migration series and not the boundary; the other capability's tables stay reachable on the same connection.
- **Keeping the foreign keys across the two files** — rejected: the constraint is the cross-capability join ADR-0003 forbids.
- **Shared metadata (persons, organisations) as a third capability beside project metadata** — *deferred*: the only shared-entity writes today are entity proposals, and their decisions are applied inside the approve transaction (`areas/deposit/editor/store/src/review_rounds.rs`); it becomes a candidate when shared entities get a lifecycle of their own outside a project's submission.
- **The SMTP transport in `identity-web` or in `deposit-server`** — rejected: a `lettre` dependency in a views-and-routes crate, or adapter logic at the composition root.

Not yet in code: no capability beside identity and editor; data modelling and upload are to arrive as capabilities of their own (not scheduled).

Enforced by: the two database files, each opened by one store crate (**structure**); `.github/scripts/check-capability-deps.sh` over `cargo metadata`, run by `just check`, for every edge between the area's crates but the root's (**static-analysis**); `.github/scripts/check-composition-root-deps.sh`, which holds `deposit-server` to no `core` crate (**static-analysis**). The target for the crate edges is Bazel `visibility` (ADR-0001), **structure**.
